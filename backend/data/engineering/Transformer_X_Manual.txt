Transformer X Technical Manual
Section 4.3: Electrical Specifications
The insulation class for Transformer X is Class F (155°C). Ensure winding temperature does not exceed 115°C under nominal loading conditions.
Recommended torque for terminal bolts in MCC Panel A is 45 Nm. Under no circumstances should the torque exceed 55 Nm to prevent copper threads stripping.
Relay protection is configured using standard IEEE 51 (Inverse Time Overcurrent) and IEEE 50 (Instantaneous Overcurrent) protection curves.
