1.0 WARRANTY CLAUSES & EXTENDED SERVICE AGREEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section governs the commercial warranty rules for all Siemens-Alstom industrial
power distribution cabinets, switchgears, transformers, and electronic controllers.

Q1.1: What is the standard manufacturer warranty period for heavy equipment?
A: All primary equipment, including Gas-Insulated Switchgears (GIS), vacuum breakers, 
and power distribution panels (e.g., heavy-duty MCC Panels), is covered by a standard 
36-month (3 years) manufacturer warranty. This warranty period commences either 
from the date of dispatch from our manufacturing facilities or 24 months from the 
installation sign-off, whichever occurs first.

Q1.2: What is the warranty period for programmable logic controllers and modems?
A: Compact electronic control modules, PLC chips, communications cards, and Modbus
gateways are covered by a standard 24-month (2 years) warranty period starting
from the date of installation.

Q1.3: Can customers purchase warranty extensions?
A: Yes. Extended warranty packages are available for purchase during the original
warranty window.
- Extended Warranty Plus: Extends total coverage to 60 months (5 years) and includes
  annual calibration and oil degradation tests for transformers.
- Ultimate Protection: Covers up to 120 months (10 years) for primary substation
  equipment and includes guaranteed 24-hour on-site technician response.

2.0 SHIPPING, CLAIMS & SPARE PARTS PROCESSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We maintain dedicated spare parts centers in Houston, Munich, Singapore, and Mumbai.

Q2.1: How do I request emergency replacement parts?
A: To initiate an emergency replacement, send an email to logistics@siemens-alstom.com.
You must attach the following:
- original Purchase Order (PO) number
- Equipment Serial Number (e.g., SN-MCCB-99420)
- SCADA telemetry fault logs (specifically current, voltage, and thermal curves)
- High-resolution photographs showing the burnt or carbonized terminal components.

Q2.2: What is the return and refund policy for auxiliary accessories?
A: Standard catalog items (mounting brackets, DIN rail components, Modbus adaptors,
PN-200 communications cards) can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of
delivery. The items must be shipped back in their original, unopened packaging.
Custom-designed busbars, copper splices, and painted enclosure sheets are non-refundable.

3.0 FIELD SUPPORT & commission SERVICE CHANNELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We operate a tier-based global technical dispatch system for field assistance.

Q3.1: How quickly can a field technician be dispatched to a site?
A: Service dispatch speeds depend on the customer's service level agreement (SLA):
- Tier 1 (Critical Infrastructure): Under "Ultimate Protection" contract, on-site arrival is guaranteed within 12 hours for domestic locations and 24 hours globally.
- Tier 2 (Standard Contract): Arrival within 24 hours domestic; 48 hours international.
- Tier 3 (Non-Contract): Field engineer dispatch is subject to availability, with typical lead times of 3 to 5 business days.

Q3.2: How do customers mount the controller mounting bracket?
A: Mount the steel controller bracket to a flat metal backplate using 4 M6 hex screws. 
Ensure the mounting surface is grounded. A minimum clearance of 50mm around all 
ventilation slots is strictly required to prevent thermal throttling.
