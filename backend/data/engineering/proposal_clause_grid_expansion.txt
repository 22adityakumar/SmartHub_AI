1.0 TENDER GENERAL TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS & SYSTEM SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes the standard technical design criteria and compliance clauses
for substation sales bids, commercial proposals, and tender specifications.

Clause 1.1: General Scope of Works
The contractor shall perform the design, detailed engineering, manufacturing, testing
at factory, safe transport, installation of civil foundation pads, on-site erection,
commissioning, and integration for regional grid expansion projects.
- Standard Substation Capacity: 45MVA.
- Configuration: Wye-Delta step-down transformers (33kV to 11kV primary distribution).
- Frequency: 50Hz / 60Hz nominal operations.

Clause 1.2: Civil Engineering & Site Work Guidelines
All civil designs must conform to substation safety codes:
- Transformer Foundation Pads: Reinforced concrete pads, class C30 mix, designed to withstand dry weight of 65 metric tons per transformer.
- Oil Soak Pits: A concrete catchment bunding wall must surround the transformer base, capable of containing 110% of the insulation mineral oil volume. In the event of catastrophic rupture, drainage channels must direct oil to a centralized separator soak pit.
- Separation Firewalls: A reinforced firewall must separate adjacent transformers. The firewall must possess a minimum 4-hour fire rating, measuring at least 4 meters in height and 300mm in thickness.

2.0 SWITCHGEAR ARCHITECTURE & CIRCUIT CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Electrical isolation, busbar layouts, and primary protection controls.

Clause 2.1: Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS) Specifications
Primary switchrooms must utilize Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS) units to minimize
physical footprint and maximize environment-independent reliability.
- Insulation Gas: Sulfur Hexafluoride (SF6) gas.
- SF6 Pressure Alert Limits: Nominal 0.55 MPa; Warning alarm low at 0.45 MPa; Critical low trip threshold at 0.42 MPa.
- Short-Circuit Withstand Capability: Rated for 31.5kA short-circuit withstand for 3.0 seconds.
- Busbar Rating: Copper busbars rated at 2000A continuous, gold-plated contacts.

Clause 2.2: Redundancy & Communication Interfaces
- Relay Protection: Dual-redundant numerical relays configured with standard IEEE 50/51 overcurrent curves, and IEEE 87T differential transformer protection.
- Communications Interface: All nodes must interface with SCADA systems using dual-redundant fiber optic rings running the IEC 61850 protocol.
- Interlocking Circuits: Mechanical key interlocks must be installed to prevent isolator disconnects under live loads.
